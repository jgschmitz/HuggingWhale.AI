# HuggingWhale example handbook

The demo support team operates from Chicago. Support hours are Monday through
Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM Central Time. Urgent issues use the on-call escalation path.

An employee may expense up to $75 per month for approved learning materials.
Manager approval is required before the purchase, and receipts must be submitted
within 30 days. This is synthetic demo data, not a real company's policy.
